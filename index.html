Important matching rules

  • Markup is determined from the Online file's Category, not the ERP category.
  • Unique ERP UOM MIX → automatic calculation.
  • Duplicate ERP UOM MIX → Manual Review; price is not changed.
  • No ERP match → No ERP Match; price is not changed.
  • Blank Carton Size → multiplier of 1. A numeric multiplier greater than 0 is applied.
  • Target price is rounded to the nearest price ending in 5 or 9 cents.
  • The bulk workbook starts from the uploaded Online workbook and changes only Price cells in Column F for safe unique matches.
